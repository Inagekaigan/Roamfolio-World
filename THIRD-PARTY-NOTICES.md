# Third-party notices

## World city directory

City names, administrative regions and geographic identifiers come from GeoNames through the datasets/world-cities project. Roamfolio reformats the CSV into an offline JavaScript index. The data is distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

https://www.geonames.org/  
https://github.com/datasets/world-cities  
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Japanese municipality directory

Roamfolio reformats the localgovlistjp municipal directory into searchable records. Romanized place names come from Japan Post's KEN_ALL_ROME.CSV. Municipal website addresses come from code4fukui/localgovjp (CC0). The localgovlistjp project applies CC0 to its own contributions.

https://github.com/kebhr/localgovlistjp  
https://www.post.japanpost.jp/zipcode/dl/readme_ro.html  
https://github.com/code4fukui/localgovjp  
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## Map

The offline base map uses the world GeoJSON from the D3 Graph Gallery, transformed to a projected SVG path.

https://github.com/holtzy/D3-graph-gallery/blob/master/DATA/world.geojson

## Online guides and images

City guides retrieved at runtime use Wikipedia content. The app links each guide to the source article and its revision history. Text excerpts may be shortened. Wikipedia text is available under CC BY-SA subject to the source article's terms. Wikimedia images have individual licenses on their file pages. The app also loads destination photographs from Unsplash.

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyrights  
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/  
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia  
https://unsplash.com/license
